— The problem

Without omabox, an agent testing a desktop app tests it on your desktop.

Windows land on your editor, your keystrokes go into its app, your cursor jumps. Here is the same agent, twice.

Without omabox your desktop

  1. Its app opens on top of your editor
  2. Your next keystrokes go into its app
  3. Your workspace switches to the agent's
  4. Your cursor jumps to its button
  5. A password prompt waits for you
  6. Its test notifications stay in your bar
3windows2focus1workspace4cursor1prompt2left

With omabox its box

  1. Its app opens in its box
  2. It types into its box's own keyboard
  3. Its workspaces are the box's
  4. Its pointer is the box's
  5. pkexec must be setuid root: no prompt reaches you
  6. Its notifications go with the box

— How you use it

You ask. Your agent takes a box.

Install once. A skill your agent already loads tells it when a task needs a desktop. You don't change your projects or your prompts.

Check the new settings dialog and show me.

It starts a box, runs your build, opens the dialog, reads it, takes a shot. The box goes when it's done. You kept typing the whole time.

$ omabox up box 'app-5cc72cdc' up (1920x1080@60) $ omabox run -d -- ./build/app $ omabox click --window app 640 380 --wait satisfied: window opened, focused: app "Settings" $ omabox tree --find 'dark mode' $ omabox shot --window app
Start a box with the app and the dev server running. I want to try it.

A window opens on workspace 9 without taking your focus: a desktop you drive. Say “make the sidebar narrower” and it rebuilds the app in that same box while you watch.

$ omabox up --interactive $ omabox run -d -- npm run dev $ omabox run -d -- ./build/app you: make the sidebar narrower $ make && omabox run -d --replace -- ./build/app
Try all three branches.

Three agents, three worktrees, three boxes, side by side. None sees another's screen, and none sees yours.

# worktree agent its box # sidebar claude app-sidebar-5cc72cdc # menu codex app-menu-9f01d2e4 # keyring opencode app-keyring-3b7a6c11

Every command is yours to type too. But omabox is built to be driven by an agent: one call per step, answers in text, waits instead of sleeps. Even to drive a box yourself, the fastest way in is to ask.

— Built for agents

Everything an agent needs to test its work, and nothing of yours.

Parallel

One box per session

Named after its worktree and session. 400 to 700 MB, up in 3 to 5 s, down when the agent exits. Subagents get a box and a brief each.

Answers in text

— Born on Omarchy

Fullest on Omarchy.

On Omarchy a box is your desktop: Omarchy's own config and shell, your theme and bar, your plugins with --desk-shell, and a widget in your bar that lists every box.

omabox on Omarchy →

— What it is not

It does one job: keep agents off your desktop.

Not a sandbox

It is not a security boundary: a box shares your kernel, GPU and network. Pair it with ai-jail.

Not for your own desktop

For an agent beside you on yours, see Cua.

Not a whole machine

For an installer or a reboot, use a VM, like omarchy-in-omarchy.

Give every agent a desktop of its own.
