How you use it

You ask. Your agent takes a box.

Install once. A skill your agent already loads tells it when a task needs a desktop. You don't change your projects or your prompts.

Every command is yours to type too. But omabox is built to be driven by an agent: one call per step, answers in text, waits instead of sleeps. Even to drive a box yourself, the fastest way in is to ask.

    


    

      

      
Built for agents
Everything an agent needs to test its work, and nothing of yours.

      

        
One box per session
Named after its worktree and session. 400 to 700 MB, up in 3 to 5 s, down when the agent exits. Subagents get a box and a brief each.
$ omabox brief -b app-5cc72cdc
# the lines a subagent needs, not the whole skill

Born on Omarchy
Fullest on Omarchy.
On Omarchy a box is your desktop: Omarchy's own config and shell, your theme and bar, your plugins with --desk-shell, and a widget in your bar that lists every box.
Click the mark.
omabox on Omarchy →

      

    


    

      

      
What it is not
It does one job: keep agents off your desktop.

      

        
Not a sandbox
It is not a security boundary: a box shares your kernel, GPU and network. Pair it with ai-jail.

        
Not for your own desktop
For an agent beside you on yours, see Cua.

        
Not a whole machine
For an installer or a reboot, use a VM, like omarchy-in-omarchy.
