Agents, off your desktop

Your agents get desktops of their own. Yours stays untouched.

omabox gives every AI agent a whole Omarchy desktop, invisible and in parallel, to launch, click, type and screenshot apps in. However much it does, it never takes control of your system.

  • No windows popping up out of nowhere
  • Your cursor never moves
  • No focus stolen mid-sentence
  • No password or keyring prompts
  • No notifications or tray icons left behind
  • No workspace switches
git clone https://github.com/diogochaves/omabox && cd omabox && ./install.sh

Omarchy 4 · Hyprland 0.56+ · an AMD, Intel or NVIDIA GPU · MIT

Parallel boxes

      
One box per agent, the way you give each one a worktree.

      
A worktree keeps agents' code apart. A box keeps their screens, session buses and test runs apart. Each agent
        session gets its own box, named after its worktree and its session, so visual checks and desktop tests run side by side
        without ever seeing each other.

    

    

    

      
      Each box: about 500 MB, up in about 4 s, down when its agent exits.

What it is not

      
It does one job: keep agents off your desktop.

      
For anything else, other projects do it well, and we point you to them.

    

    

      
Not a sandbox
A box shares your kernel, your GPU and, by default, your network. It is not a security boundary. To limit what an agent can read, write and reach, run it in ai-jail; the two stack.

        ai-jail with omabox →

      
Not for your own desktop
omabox keeps agents off your desktop. If you want an agent working beside you on yours, look at Cua.

        How they compare →

      
Not a whole machine
A box has no system bus, installer or reboot. For those, omarchy-in-omarchy runs a whole Omarchy in a VM.

        How they compare →

      
Only for Omarchy
A box runs your own Omarchy: its Hyprland config, its shell and your theme. That is what makes it faithful, and why it needs Omarchy 4 on Arch.

Questions

      
Questions people ask first

      
Does it work without Omarchy?
No. A box runs your host's own Omarchy: its Hyprland config, its shell and your theme. That is what makes it faithful, and why it needs Omarchy 4 and Hyprland 0.56 or later.

      
Is omabox part of Omarchy?
No. omabox is an independent community project, built by the people below, and is not made or endorsed by the Omarchy project. It is built for Omarchy and follows its conventions, so report problems on omabox's GitHub, not to Omarchy.

      
Can two agents test at the same time?
Yes. Each Claude Code or Codex session gets its own box, named after its repo or worktree and its session. Anything else started with omabox guard exec gets one too.

      
Is it a sandbox?
No. It keeps apps off your desktop and never gets your secrets, input devices or seat, but it shares your kernel, GPU and, unless you pass --net isolated, your network. For a fence around the agent, use ai-jail, which pairs well with omabox; for hostile code, a VM.

      
Which GPUs does it work on?
AMD, Intel and NVIDIA. On NVIDIA, headless boxes need a fix to aquamarine that install.sh builds for you; What you need has the details.

      
Does peeking slow the agent down?
No. Peek only copies frames out of the box.
