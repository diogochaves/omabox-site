omabox on Omarchy

Born on Omarchy, fullest there.

omabox started as a way to test Omarchy plugins without touching the bar you work in. On Omarchy, a box is Omarchy: its own Hyprland config and shell (bar, menu, tray, notifications), with your theme, bar layout and terminal settings. What's on this page is Omarchy's alone.

Plugins

Your bar, in a box.

Check a plugin against the bar you really use, or alone in one that isn't yours. Your own bar never changes while you work.

--desk-shell

Your whole shell

up --desk-shell brings every plugin in ~/.config/omarchy/plugins, your shell.json as it is, and the files your plugins read. Never api-keys.env, nor anything named or holding a key, token, secret or password. up lists what went in and what didn't, and why.

$ omabox up --desk-shell --plugin ~/code/myplugin   # your working copy wins

--plugin

One plugin, in a bar that isn't yours

--plugin mounts it read-only and says why the shell didn't load it: a manifest it refuses, a QML error. --stock-bar shows it in Omarchy's default bar, as most people will see it.

$ omabox up --plugin ~/code/myplugin
$ omabox restart-shell   # after each edit
$ omabox log shell --grep qml -n 20

Settings

Its settings, said

A box gets your plugins' settings; up names each one a plugin reads, and --no-seed extensions starts a box as a new Omarchy user.

$ omabox up --plugin ~/code/myplugin --no-seed extensions

tree --layer

Read the bar as text

tree --layer omarchy-bar reads a panel's or plugin's accessibility tree, for items that set a role. (Omarchy's own bar publishes none today; a plugin can.)

$ omabox tree --layer omarchy-bar

With an agent

Steps written for your desktop, done in a box

Your project's instructions say "link the plugin into ~/.config/omarchy/plugins", "edit shell.json", "run omarchy plugin add". The omabox skill has the agent do each of them in its box instead: those are file writes, and your bar would change at once.

Themes

Your theme, live.

--theme-dir puts the theme you are editing in the box, every save with it; --theme starts a box on the theme you name, whatever your desktop is on. omarchy-theme-set in the box applies an edit.

$ omabox up --theme-dir ~/code/my-theme --theme my-theme
$ omabox run -- omarchy-theme-set my-theme   # after an edit
$ omabox up stock --stock-bar --theme tokyo-night

Checks

A clean Omarchy, for checks.

up --clean: Omarchy's bar on tokyo-night, a solid magenta wallpaper (any of it inside a window is a hole), the bar's clock frozen at Friday 12:00, English in UTC, no pointer, no network, no fonts of yours, no animations. A baseline is the same image on every machine. --keep takes back what a test needs.

$ omabox up --clean
$ omabox up --clean --keep fonts,pointer

Omarchy itself

Omarchy's own checkout and stack.

--omarchy

Your checkout, never installed

up --omarchy DIR runs a box on that tree's config, shell and scripts, as omarchy dev link would, without touching your system.

$ omabox up dev --omarchy ~/src/omarchy
$ omabox hyprctl reload

--quickshell

Your Quickshell build

up --quickshell PATH runs the box's shell on a Quickshell build of yours.

$ omabox up --quickshell ~/code/quickshell/build/src/quickshell

Binds and tests

Omarchy's own binds work

The browser bind opens the browser in the box, the shell restart restarts the box's shell. Omarchy's own shell tests pass in one.

The bar widget

Every box, from your bar.

The omabox mark in your bar lists every box, with peek, screenshot and down for each, keys-to-box and the clipboard for an interactive one, and an orphan box (one nobody will take down) said on its row.

The copy here works: click a box, or focus the panel and use its keys. Its gear opens the settings.

$ omabox down --orphans
