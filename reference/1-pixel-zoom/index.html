0.2.0 for Omarchy · Hyprland

A desktop of their own, for your agents.

omabox gives an AI agent a whole Omarchy desktop to launch, drive and screenshot apps in, invisible and apart from yours. No windows on your screen, no stolen focus, no cursor jumps, nothing left in your session.

git clone https://github.com/diogochaves/omabox && cd omabox && ./install.sh

Omarchy 4 · Hyprland 0.56+ · a GPU render node · MIT
